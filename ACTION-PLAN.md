# Kreative Reflow: 14-Day Client & Content Action Plan

Print this or keep it open every day. Tick boxes as you go. If a day goes wrong, don't restart the plan, just continue from where you are.

**The two goals:**
1. Sign 1 or 2 paying clients in the next 14 days (deposit in your account).
2. Have content running daily so next month's outreach is warmer.

**The one rule:** outreach comes before everything else. Before the dashboard, before polishing the site, before new features. No money, no business.

---

## STEP 0: Set up your offer (do this once, today, ~1 hour)

Write these numbers down and stick to them. Fill in the blanks now, not "later":

- [ ] Founding client price for a funeral parlour website with online registration + payment:  R________
  (This is your discounted rate. Never call it "half price" to a client. Call it a **founding client rate**, limited to 3 funeral parlours, in exchange for a testimonial.)
- [ ] Deposit to start: 50% = R________
- [ ] Monthly fee after launch (hosting, support, small updates): R________ /month
- [ ] Quick-cash side offer: Google Business Profile setup/fix = R________ (suggest R750 to R1,500, delivered in 1 day)

**Why this structure:** the deposit pays your WiFi this month. The monthly fee means you are never this broke again. Funeral parlours already run on monthly premiums, so they understand monthly billing better than any other industry.

**Stay on the right side of the law:** you sell the website, registration flow, and payment plumbing. You do NOT sell or advise on funeral cover itself (that's FSCA regulated). If a client asks about cover rules, that's their compliance officer's job, not yours.

## DAY 1: Build your weapons (full day)

### Morning: the demo video
- [ ] Record a 90-second screen recording of your funeral parlour demo, with your voice over it.
- [ ] Script to follow while recording:
  1. "This is what your members see." Show the homepage.
  2. "Someone in another town registers here." Show the registration form.
  3. "They upload their ID and documents here."
  4. "They choose a plan and pay here." Show the payment step.
  5. "And you see everything in your dashboard here."
- [ ] Keep it under 2 minutes. Don't re-record more than 3 times. Done beats perfect.
- [ ] Upload it somewhere you can share with one link (YouTube unlisted works fine).
- [ ] Save the link here: ________________________________

### Afternoon: the prospect sheet
- [ ] Create a Google Sheet called "Prospects" with these columns:
  | Name | Town | Facebook page | Website? (none / old / decent) | Phone/WhatsApp | Pain I saw (be specific) | Status | Last contact date |
- [ ] Fill it using these sources, town by town (start Gauteng, then widen):
  - Google Maps: search "funeral parlour", "funeral services", "burial society" + town name
  - Facebook: search same terms, open each page
  - NAFUPA SA and other funeral association member lists
- [ ] For every prospect, write ONE specific pain in the sheet. Examples: "Someone asked 'where are you based' on 14 May, no answer." / "No website at all." / "Website has no way to join online."
- [ ] Screenshot unanswered Facebook questions when you see them. Save them in a folder. Each screenshot is a personalized pitch.
- [ ] Target: **50 prospects on the sheet by tonight.** If you hit 30 and you're exhausted, fine, finish the rest tomorrow morning.

## DAY 2 ONWARDS: The daily rhythm (Mon-Fri, ~4 hours minimum)

Do these four blocks every weekday. Tick them off daily in the tracker at the bottom.

### Block 1 - Morning (45 min): Feed the sheet
- [ ] Add 10 new prospects with a specific pain noted for each.

### Block 2 - Midday (90 min): Send messages
- [ ] Send 10 to 15 first-touch messages. WhatsApp first. If no WhatsApp number, Facebook DM. Phone call third. Email last resort.
- [ ] Personalize the FIRST LINE of every message using the pain from your sheet. Never send the template raw.
- [ ] Update Status column: "Contacted" + today's date.

**First-touch template (edit the bracketed parts):**

> Hi [name], I was on your Facebook page and noticed someone asked how to join from [town] and there wasn't a way for them to do it online. I build websites for funeral parlours where people anywhere in SA can register, upload their documents and pay, all from their phone. I built a working demo you can watch here: [demo video link]. Would you be open to a quick call this week? I'm [your name] from Kreative Reflow in Joburg.

### Block 3 - Afternoon (60 min): Follow-ups and calls
- [ ] Check the sheet: anyone contacted 2-3 days ago with no reply gets the follow-up message.
- [ ] Anyone contacted 7+ days ago with no reply after follow-up: mark "Closed for now" and move on. No third chasing message.
- [ ] Do any calls that got booked.

**Follow-up template:**

> Hi [name], just checking you saw my last message. The short version: families who live far from your branch currently can't become members without travelling to you. I can fix that. The demo is here if you want a look: [link]. Happy to show you on a 10 minute call.

### Block 4 - Late afternoon (60 min): Content
- [ ] Post 1 short video (from your batch, see Content section below) to Facebook Reels, TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts. Same clip on all of them.
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM on your channels.

## WHEN SOMEONE REPLIES (the part that makes money)

### If they're interested:
- [ ] Push for a 10-minute call (voice or video). Don't try to close the deal in text.
- [ ] On the call: ask about THEIR business first. How do people currently join? How far do members live? How do they pay now? Then show the demo and connect it to what they just told you.
- [ ] End the call with: "I'll send you a one-page summary on WhatsApp today."

### The one-page offer (send same day, on WhatsApp):
No long proposals. Type this out or make a simple PDF:
- [ ] What they get: 5 or 6 bullets (website, online registration, document upload, payment, admin dashboard, training)
- [ ] Founding client price: R________
- [ ] Deposit to start: R________
- [ ] Launch date: ________ (give yourself 3 to 4 weeks)
- [ ] Monthly fee after launch: R________
- [ ] One line: "Founding rate is in exchange for a testimonial and case study. I'm only taking 3 funeral parlours at this rate."

### If they say "not now" or "no budget":
- [ ] Offer the side deal: "No problem. One small thing that would help you immediately: I can fix your Google Maps listing so people searching 'funeral parlour near me' actually find you. R________, done in a day."

### If they go quiet after the offer:
- [ ] One follow-up after 3 days: "Hi [name], any questions on the summary I sent? Happy to adjust the launch date if timing is the issue."
- [ ] Then leave it. Mark the sheet and move on.

## CONTENT: One filming day per week

Pick one day (Saturday works). Batch-film 5 clips of 45 to 90 seconds. Phone camera is fine. Then post one per weekday in Block 4.

### Rules for every clip:
- Vertical video, talk to the camera or record your screen.
- First sentence is the hook. No intros, no "hey guys welcome back."
- End every clip with ONE call to action, rotating between these:
  - "Free scorecard, link in bio" (your website tools)
  - "Click through the demo yourself, link in bio"
  - "WhatsApp me the word LEAK and I'll look at your site"

### Week 1 filming list (in this order):
- [ ] Clip 1: "I looked at 20 funeral parlour Facebook pages. Almost every one had the same unanswered question in the comments." Tell the story, show (blurred) screenshots.
- [ ] Clip 2: Demo walkthrough: "How someone in Cape Town joins a funeral parlour in Limpopo in 4 minutes, documents and payment included."
- [ ] Clip 3: "Your Facebook page is not a website. Here's what the difference is costing you."
- [ ] Clip 4: Teardown of an old (anonymous) service business website: "Here's where this site loses customers." Tone is helpful, never mocking.
- [ ] Clip 5: "What a website really costs in South Africa in 2026." (Use your own article. This one will get comments and arguments. Good.)

### Week 2 filming list:
- [ ] Clip 6: "You don't need a new website. You need these 3 fixes." (rebuild vs refresh)
- [ ] Clip 7: "I asked ChatGPT to recommend a funeral parlour in [town]. Here's who it named and why yours wasn't there."
- [ ] Clip 8: Fix a Google Business Profile live in 5 minutes.
- [ ] Clip 9: "Why your website looks good but doesn't convert." (from your article)
- [ ] Clip 10: "The enquiry you answered 6 hours late already hired someone else." (lead response calculator)

### LinkedIn (2 posts per week, Tuesday and Thursday):
- [ ] Write the text version of your two best clips that week. 150 to 250 words, same hook, end with the same CTA.

## WHAT TO TAKE OFF YOUR PLATE (just as important)

Do NOT spend time on these for the next 14 days:

- ❌ Polishing the dashboard/portal. Improve it only after outreach blocks are done for the day.
- ❌ Redesigning anything on the website. It launches Monday as-is. It's good enough to sell from.
- ❌ Writing new insights articles. You have five. Repurpose them into clips instead.
- ❌ Long proposals, pricing pages, brochures, company profiles. The one-pager is enough.
- ❌ Cold email campaigns. Wrong channel for this audience. WhatsApp and Facebook only.
- ❌ Paid ads. No budget, and you don't need them yet.
- ❌ New tools, new tech, new plugins, new ideas. Park them in a "later" note.
